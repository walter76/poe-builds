# Path of Exile 2 - Tips for a Faster Campaign

- Town is lava - __don't enter town without a purpose__
- __Know and learn the build ahead of time__ (this prevents idle time questioning
  everything).
- __Use a Regex at the vendor for the build__
- __Use a loot filter for the build,__ hide loot you do not need. Stay focused. 
  [FilterBlade - PoE 2](https://www.filterblade.xyz/?game=Poe2)
- __Always keep the weapon upgraded,__ max resistances and life when possible.
- __Good enough is better than optimized.__
- Zone pathing order + **what to skip / come back to**
- **Try to kill magic monsters to maintain xp,** rares take too long and white
  mobs don't give extra XP. **Magic monsters are efficient.**

## Gear/Items

- **Don't waste currency** on soon to be replaced gear, but **keep your weapons upgraded**:
  use Aug/Trans.
- **Remember:** Always check if modifiers on the item apply **to the item or globally.**
  Most of the time they only apply to the item, e.g. #% increased physical
  damage.
- **Know gear and weapon breakpoints** (base type + item level for next tier
  affixes). [Craft of Exile](https://www.craftofexile.com/)
- **Gear into bosses resistance wise.** (see Appendix: Boss Resistance Cheat
  Sheet)
- **Upgrade gear immediately when things start to feel weird.** Don't wait too
  long.
- You could check vendors in previous acts if desperate, but the iLvl generally
  will be lower.

## Gold and Currency

Early in the campaign you are low on money and currency.

- Disenchant unidentified or low-value items.
- Sell Rares for money.
- Buy Scroll of Wisdom.

## Experience Penalty Breakpoints

**Maintain level and try to stay at the low end of the xp penalty cap** (i.e.
you are always +1 away from being penalized). This way you are fighting monsters
that grant the most XP whilst receiving 100 % XP.

PoE 2 awards full XP only when a monster's level is within a certain gap of your
character level. That gap (the "safe window") widens as you level up - at low
levels it's +/- 3, growing to +/- 6 by level 48+. Inside the window you get
100 % XP per kill, outside it, XP drops off sharply. The optimization: enter
each act/zone at the low end of its bracket so monsters are slightly above you
(bonus XP) while still inside the safe window.

| **Player Lvl** | **Area Min** | **Area Max** | **Change Occurs At** |
|----------------|--------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **1 to 15** | -3 | +3 | ACT I - Throughout |
| **16 to 31** | -4 | +4 | ACT II - Vastiri Desert (16) |
| **32 to 47** | -5 | +5 | ACT III - Dreadnought Vanguard (32) |
| **48 to 63** | -6 | +6 | Act IV - 2nd Island (48) |

> _Note: the "Change Occurs At" column is the level gap (your level vs.
> monster level) at which the XP penalty kicks in - not a monster level. So at
> character level 20 (bracket 16-31), a monster 4+ levels away from you starts
> giving reduced XP._
