# Mechanics

- Do __Abyss__ to get:
  - __Abyss crafting currency__
  - General currency
- Do __Ritual__ to get:
  - __Specific Unique items__
  - __Omens__
- Do __Temple__ to get:
  - Vaal currency
  - Irradiated tablets
  - General currency
  - Vaal orbs
  - __Atziri special supports__
  - __Double corruption__
  - __Mechanical limbs (lost on death)__
  - Lineage gems
- Do __Delirium__ to get:
  - __Amulet instilling__
  - __Very Large Radius on timeless jewel__
  - __Emotion crafting for gems__
  - __Double instilled amulets__
  - __Effect of Prefix / Suffix mod liquids__
- Do __Expedition__ to get:
  - __Runestones__
  - __+1 permanent level unlock__

# Trials
