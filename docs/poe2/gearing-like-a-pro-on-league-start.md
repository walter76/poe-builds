## Step 1 Farming

### How do I Farm?

Key breakpoints on gear when the most important affixes/modifiers appear:

| Tier | Level | Waystone | Notable Affixes / Slots |
|------|-------|----------|-------------------------|
| 1 | 65 | Ancient Waystone 1 | **Boots** — 30% Movement Speed<br>**Helmets** — +2 Minion / Critical Hit Chance<br>**Gloves** — +2 Melee / Critical Damage Bonus / Attack Speed |
| 7 | 71 | Refined Waystone 2 | Up to 40% resistances on all gear slots — hunt for Belts, Armour, and Rings.<br>**Armour** also gains extra defence stats at this tier. |
| 11 | 75 | Pristine Waystone 1 | Most maximum flat damage modifiers become available.<br>+3 gear starts dropping. |

> We are spending more time in Tier 1 gearing unitl we can make the jump to be comfortable in Tier 7, and then the same
> to jump to Tier 11. __Farming maps in the middle isn't as productive as extra difficulty doesn't mean better rewards
> in these cases.
