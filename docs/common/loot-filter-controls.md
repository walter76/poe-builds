Loot filter controls are the client hotkeys used to interact with item labels shown on the ground, which are governed by your active [[loot-filter]].

- **Alt (Hold)**: Temporarily shows all item labels on the ground that match or are hidden by the filter.
- **Z (Toggle)**: Toggles item labels completely on or off for the items currently permitted by your filter.
